--- layout: post title: Building firmware for the ASUS DSL-N12E-C1 modem-router --- A few months ago, I recovered an ASUS DSL-N12E-C1 modem-router combo someone was throwing away. At the time I didn't have a router at home, so I picked it up; it worked flawlessly first time. I then recefound out ASUS had published the full source code for the router firmware, so I started figuring out how to build it.
First of all, I had to settle on an OS. In the readme, ASUS says to use either Ubuntu 10.04 or CentOS 6.2; I decided to go with Ubuntu since I was familiar with it.
