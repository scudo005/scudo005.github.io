--- layout: post title: 'PSA: Archive your stuff' --- Please, if you have any software you used (or used) extensively, be it games, drivers, digital school books, boring office software, whatever, check if it's been uploaded to archive.org, even if you think it's stupid or irrelevant. It will be relevant for somebody else in the future, and it costs you nothing.

And please, if you find something that hasn't been archived yet, archive it. (Contact me if you don't know how.)
