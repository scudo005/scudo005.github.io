--- layout: post title: Morals or not --- Sometimes I find myself thinking about all the people that complain about a thing, swearing they despise it and they hate it and they wish it never existed, and then they proceed to make use of the very thing they were just complaining about. There has been a lot of talk in the last few years about how people think AI is unethical and produces sloppy worthless output, yet I rarely see these people not use AI despite they think that it is unethical garbage, and despite them not being forced by anything to use it (for example, their job). I guess your own rules are as strong as your willpower to enforce them.
